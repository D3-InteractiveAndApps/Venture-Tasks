Client work

Tag tasks with a client and a value (in a task’s ⋯ details), then export them here as a spreadsheet — for an invoice, a quarterly tax estimate, or just to see what a client’s worth.

Appearance

Pick one of 20 built-in themes for an instant, cohesive look, or pick a font and override individual colors yourself below — either way, every field stays editable afterward.

Your data

Deleted something by mistake? Deleted tasks, sections, and pages are kept for 30 days.

Export a backup, or move your sections and tasks to another browser or computer.

Export your due dates as a calendar file to see them alongside everything else on your phone or desktop calendar. It's a one-time snapshot, not a live feed — re-export whenever you want it to catch up.

Live calendar

A subscribe link instead of a file: add it once to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook as a "subscription" calendar and it keeps itself up to date — no re-exporting. It's the same due dates as the export above, plus anything on a shared list you're a member of, so everyone subscribed sees the same live calendar.

AI co-pilot optional

Off by default, and nothing else in the app needs it. If you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini, you can connect your own API key for task breakdowns, draft replies, and a weekly review. Your key is stored only in this browser and sent directly from your browser to that provider — it never passes through anything else.
